Orbit & Axle — Issue #2

Week of October 25–31, 2026 · About a 5-minute read

Hi from the Orbit & Axle crew. Welcome to issue #2. Each week we round up what happened across Tesla, SpaceX, xAI, X, Neuralink and The Boring Company, with a link for every item. This week: a big speech-implant number, a Starship nearly ready to fly, the 700th Falcon 9 and a quiet change to Tesla's referrals.

The big one: Neuralink reports 75 words a minute from thought

On Oct. 28, Neuralink posted early data from VOICE, its speech-restoration study. One participant with ALS reached 75 words per minute with a 5.8% word error rate on a 125,000-word vocabulary, in their first week. Principal investigator Dr. Nader Pouratian presented the results that day at the annual NEALS meeting.

A day later, Neuralink described a 72-hour "hackathon" at a VOICE participant's home. Engineers built automatic detection of when he's trying to speak (no more button), an iOS dictation keyboard, and a way to feed decoded speech straight into iPhone calls. One prototype let him trigger a Grok agent by thinking "Hey Neuralink" plus a task.

Why it matters: Inside BCI says that's faster than published academic results on the same vocabulary. But it's company-reported, covers one week, isn't peer-reviewed, and Neuralink hasn't said whether 75 is a peak or an average. The devices are investigational and not FDA-approved.

Sources: Neuralink on X · Neuralink blog · Inside BCI

Around the empire

SpaceX: both Flight 15 Starship stages have now fired

On Oct. 25, SpaceX posted a full-duration, 33-engine static fire of the Flight 15 Super Heavy booster. On Oct. 26 and 27, Ship 42 ran a full-duration six-engine burn and a single-Raptor firing to simulate a deorbit burn. Musk has said SpaceX will try to catch both stages on Flight 15 if regulators approve. SpaceX has never attempted a ship catch.

Sources: SpaceX on X (booster) · SpaceX on X (ship) · Space.com

SpaceX: the 700th Falcon 9 closes out October

At 12:04 a.m. ET on Oct. 31, a Falcon 9 lifted off from Vandenberg with 27 Starlink satellites: the 700th Falcon 9 launch ever, per Spaceflight Now. Booster B1093, on its 18th flight, landed on Of Course I Still Love You, the 675th Falcon booster landing. Spaceflight Now notes the pace is easing as SpaceX shifts to Starship: after Oct. 27, its 365-day launch count dipped below 150 for the first time since July 2025.

Sources: Spaceflight Now · Space.com

Starlink: prices that move with local demand

Starlink is testing "dynamic pricing" on Residential plans in parts of the U.S. Midwest. A customer email says prices are "dynamically based on supply of capacity and demand" and will be reviewed "roughly quarterly." PCMag found Missouri prices rising (one Residential plan from $50 to $60.50; Max listed at $156 for new sign-ups in Rolla, up from $130) and Max falling to $117 in Kansas City, Mo., with changes apparently capped near 20% either way.

Sources: PCMag · How-To Geek

Tesla: U.S. referrals now pay in FSD, not cars

Tesla launched a U.S. referral program for FSD (Supervised). A referred owner gets a free month; if they subscribe, the referrer gets a free month, or a $99 Tesla Credit if they bought FSD outright, up to 12 times a year. Meanwhile, buying a car no longer earns U.S. referral rewards.

Sources: Electrek · Not a Tesla App

Tesla: Seoul court rejects FSD refund suit

On Oct. 29, the Seoul Central District Court ruled against 88 owners seeking FSD refunds, who argued Tesla hadn't delivered the feature in Korea in over eight years. The court found FSD never promised fully driverless operation and that further features depend on technology and regulators. Tesla did not immediately comment, Reuters reported.

Sources: Reuters via Claims Journal · CNA

X: repost a photo, and the creator gets credit

On Oct. 27, X launched photo resharing: long-press a photo, tap "Post photo," and the original author gets credit, with both accounts earning from Original Content Rewards. On Oct. 28, X added subscriber-only group chats in beta for creators with 800 or fewer subscribers.

Sources: X Creators on X (photos) · X Creators on X (chats) · Social Media Today

The Boring Company: one control room, three cities

On Oct. 28, The Boring Company said its Global Ops Center in Bastrop, Texas, was remotely running machines in Las Vegas and Nashville (tunneling) and San Antonio (commissioning, not yet tunneling). KSAT reports the San Antonio machine, Prufrock-5, arrived Oct. 14 at Morgan's Inclusion Campus for a nearly mile-long tunnel and awaits final approval.

Sources: Boring Co. on X · KSAT

Rumour vs confirmed

🟡 Going around: "Blindsight is ready, just waiting on regulators." Musk posted this just after midnight ET on Oct. 30. It's his claim, not a regulatory milestone: Blindsight's FDA Breakthrough Device designation (Sept. 2024) isn't an approval, and Neuralink hasn't yet shown it restores vision in humans. Sources: Elon Musk on X · India Today

🟡 Going around: "Starship Flight 15 launches Nov. 12." That date comes from a screenshot circulating online of what's described as an air-traffic planning slide. SpaceX hasn't announced one; per Space.com, Musk has said only that it's targeted for November. Sources: SpaceX France · Space.com

🟡 Spotted, not announced: free first Robotaxi rides in Dallas and Houston. The Robotaxi app has shown new riders "First ride on us" at $0.00. Tesla hasn't announced it or published terms. Sources: Not a Tesla App · TeslaNorth

🟢 Confirmed: Neuralink's 75 WPM (as company-reported data), the Flight 15 static fires, the 700th Falcon 9, the FSD referral terms, the Seoul ruling and X's photo resharing all come from company posts or major outlets (see above).

Canadian corner 🇨🇦

Model 3 Premium is back on the page, but you still can't order it. Tesla Canada paused Model 3 Premium orders on Oct. 12, saying it had used up its allocation under Canada's China-built EV quota. On Oct. 30, both Premium trims became selectable in the configurator again, but with a "Get Updates" button instead of an order button. Prices are C$39,490 (RWD) and C$49,990 (AWD); the C$54,990 Performance is still the only orderable Model 3. Tesla's earlier notice said orders should resume when Canada issues more quota, expected March 2027.

Sources: Drive Tesla Canada · TeslaNorth, Oct. 31 · Electrek, Oct. 13

New Supercharger on Highway 7. On Oct. 28, Tesla announced a new 8-stall Supercharger in Norwood, Ont., east of Peterborough.

Sources: Tesla Charging on X · The Electric Standard
